# Limitations and Dual-Use Considerations

Required Apart appendix (short form). Expand in the PDF as needed.

## Limitations

1. **Public reconstruction only.** Events are structured from public posts and a representative HF Space embed. We do not have lab-internal telemetry or a downloadable full ~17.6k action dump.  
2. **Analyst judgments.** Checklist and Stance PASS/FAIL are criteria-bound human (or draft-script-assisted) judgments, not ground-truth labels.  
3. **Small case set.** OpenAI→HF (main), Anthropic (thin), OpenRouter (warning-shot map). Not a census of all 2026 eval incidents.  
4. **H3 is not an RCT.** Prose-vs-events comparison uses the same author family; treat as a method check, not causal proof that forensics always help.  
5. **Forecasts are unsettled.** H4/H5 predictions have settlement rules but are not yet resolved.  
6. **Gagana experiments** are mapped from her public repo / REPORT; we do not re-claim her full experimental apparatus as our weekend result.

## Dual-use

**Intended use:** help labs, evaluators, and researchers score frame/isolation failures, demand design-stance root cause, and ask whether monitoring can stop harm in time.

**We do not provide:** exploit chains, weaponizable attack steps, credentials, or instructions to reproduce the July intrusions against third parties.

**Mitigations in this artifact:** epistemic tags; LIMITS; local-only smoke (loopback); dual-use note in README; no payload or PoC attack code.

**Residual risk:** structured timelines can still inform attackers about *what was possible* in public incidents. We accept that residual risk because the same facts are already public; our addition is scoring discipline and predictions, not new offensive capability.
